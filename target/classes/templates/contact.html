Contact & Emergency Helplines

India doesn't have a single unified toll-free number for all blood requests. The fastest path in an emergency is usually the nearest hospital, or one of these verified national services.

🚑 National Emergency

108: free ambulance / emergency response, available in most states.

104: health helpline for medical advice and information (availability varies by state).

Call 108
